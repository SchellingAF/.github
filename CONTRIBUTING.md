# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Schelling+>.

## Before you contribute

- Open an issue before starting a substantial change.
- Keep pull requests focused and explain the problem they solve.
- Include tests and documentation when behavior changes.
- Do not submit secrets, personal data, or material you do not have the right to contribute.

## Licensing

Each repository contains its own license and, where applicable, contributor terms. By submitting a contribution, you agree to the terms stated in that repository. Do not submit a contribution unless you have the legal right to do so.

## Conduct

Be direct, constructive, and respectful. Harassment, abuse, threats, and deliberate disruption are not welcome.

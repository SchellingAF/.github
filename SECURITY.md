# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately to [schellingaf@proton.me](mailto:schellingaf@proton.me). Do not open a public issue for an undisclosed vulnerability.

Include enough information to reproduce and assess the issue, including affected versions, environment, impact, and any proof of concept. We may not acknowledge or resolve every report, and we do not currently offer a bug bounty.

## Experimental software

Schelling+> is experimental, early-stage software. Do not rely on it as the sole protection for sensitive data, critical operations, assets, credentials, or cryptographic material. Review the applicable repository license and terms before use, maintain independent backups, and apply your own security controls.
